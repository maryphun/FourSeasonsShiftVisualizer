The dark shift ledger is distinctive to this product: selected-day card, restrained status colors, Japanese weekday badge, and user-supplied panda art. The conventional surrounding controls suit an operational tool. The biggest gap is behavioral: the first screen can fail to answer what the user works today.

## Overall impression

The shift value wins the eye, and desktop uses the extra width well. The largest opportunity is to make the first glance reliably land on today and communicate when today's roster is missing.

## What's working

- The selected shift is the clear focal point at 390x844 and 1440x900.
- The six-week month and totals fit the tested phone viewport without an inner scroll.
- The shift editor exposes Work/OFF, a saved-value preview, and a clear save action.

## Priority issues

1. **P1: The opening date can miss today.** With a September cached roster on October 3, the screen opened on September 1 (`1 / 30`); today's date required month navigation. Initialize the calendar to today's month and date when reachable, and provide a compact way back after browsing. Suggested command: `$impeccable harden`.
2. **P1: The no-roster state is ambiguous.** An October date showed a large dash, zeroed totals, enabled Add Event and Share, and disabled Edit with only a title explanation. Replace the dash with a clear missing-roster state and expose the existing upload action nearby while retaining next-month event entry. Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify`.
3. **P1: Phone users cannot compare shifts in the calendar.** Date cells hide their shift time while desktop shows it. Provide a compact week comparison or a phone date format that exposes shift information without making the fixed viewport scroll. Suggested command: `$impeccable adapt`.
4. **P2: Calendar tap areas are short.** Phone date rows are 36px with 4px gaps, creating a mistap risk. Increase the interactive area or use a denser view with at least 44px targets. Suggested command: `$impeccable adapt`.
5. **P2: "Leaves" overstates the total.** The implementation counts OFF, ROFF, AL, SAL, and `/` as non-working days. Rename the existing statistic to match that meaning, or count actual leave separately. Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify`.

## Persona red flags

- **Jordan, first timer:** A dash does not distinguish missing data from OFF, and `AL` is unexplained.

The current brief explicitly preserves phone zoom restriction, so that known accessibility trade-off was not treated as a newly discovered defect. Actual error, save, share, and screen-reader flows were not exercised by Assessment A.
